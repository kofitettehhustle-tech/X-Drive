From discovery to delivery

  1. Choose a path. Browse platform growth, Google search presence, or artist discovery and release services.
  2. Review the service. Check the price, required information, limits, and any service-specific timing shown on its page.
  3. Share the details. Provide the destination link and contact email requested for the service. Only submit links or content you’re authorized to use.
  4. Confirm with X Drive. Until checkout and fulfillment are connected, email support to confirm availability and next steps.

Frequently asked questions

What services does X Drive offer?

Platform growth services, Google search presence for businesses, and artist discovery and release support. Availability and requirements are shown on each service page.

Are delivery estimates guaranteed?

No. Any timing shown is an estimate and can vary by service and operating conditions. We’ll show service-specific timing on the relevant service page when it is available.

Can X Drive publish or provide music downloads?

Artist downloads can only be offered when the artist or rights holder has authorized distribution of the recording and related artwork.

How do I contact support?

Email xdrivesupport@gmail.com. Include the service name and any order reference so we can direct your request.
